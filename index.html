Features

Built like a drafting table, not another chat app.

Everything Dexterity needs from a workplace messenger, with a design language of our own: lenses instead of tabs, a spine timeline, and a floating Dock.

Spaces & Direct

Conversations that read like a revision history.

Messages hang off a single spine in runs, with day marks like a title block. Your own runs carry an amber notch, so you can find your part instantly.

  • Open and private Spaces for teams and projects
  • Direct conversations for one-to-one and small groups
  • Line numbers like 6"-P-1001-A1B are recognised automatically
Side-tracks

Side discussions stay attached to the question.

Reply in a side-track and the conversation keeps its place, visibly connected to the message that started it. Nobody loses the thread.

  • Reactions, pins, edits and deletes
  • @mentions with a personal amber highlight
  • "Seen" receipts and typing indicators
Light & dark

At home on Windows 11, by day and by night.

Mica surfaces, the system's light or dark mode, and GO TO (Ctrl+G), which jumps to any person, Space or command.

  • Pulse shows only what needs you
  • Native notifications that open the right conversation
  • Unread badge on the taskbar
Android

The same office in your pocket.

Add your phone from your PC with a QR code that installs and pairs it, no administrator needed. It joins every conversation on the office Wi-Fi, at home or on mobile data, with the same encryption.

Rooms & meetings

Meetings drawn like a sheet, not a video wall.

Every seat has a grid reference, the host's controls sit in a title block, and leaving is an amber door, not a red handset. Record with chapters for who spoke when, split into breakout rooms, or run a webinar for clients.

  • Lobby, waiting room and host controls
  • Background blur and noise suppression on the PC
  • Guests join from a browser, no install
The office drawing

An org chart you can walk through.

The office laid out as an engineering drawing: departments as areas, reporting lines as routed runs, everyone as a tagged plate that shows who is in. Pan and zoom like a CAD viewer, ask how you connect to someone, or switch to the floor plan and walk to their desk.

  • New joiners, birthdays and work anniversaries marked on the sheet
  • See who works together across Spaces
  • Replay how the office grew
Bot maker

Make your own bot in a few minutes.

Plot requests, leave notes, technical queries, site visits: pick a template or describe it in words, give it a mark, a voice and office hours, and try it beside the editor before anyone sees it. Keep it to yourself, share it with a Space, or ask for it office-wide.

  • No code; the JSON is there if you want it
  • Remembers answers ("Same site as last time?")
  • Shows where people stop, so you can fix the question
Drawings

Open an A0 sheet right in the conversation.

PDFs show their first page in the message and open in a viewer built for drawings: fit, zoom to 800%, search the text, rotate, and stay quick on large sheets. Files sent in an encrypted Direct conversation are encrypted on your PC before they leave it.

Nib & day sheets

A companion that keeps your day sheet.

Nib sits beside your tile in every app. Tell it your work in plain words, or Hinglish ("got 12 isos from Meera for P-12"), and it keeps your day sheet, then offers a pre-filled wrap-up at the end of the day. Each lead gets one card a day; management gets one register.

  • Ctrl+Alt+N opens Nib from any app
  • Never asks during a call, a meeting or Do not disturb, and three times at most
  • A leave note that sets your status, tells your leads and hands over to your cover
Notice board

Official notices, numbered and revised like drawings.

Policies, circulars, holidays and IT advisories from HR, IT, Admin and management. Each is numbered by its desk (HR-POL-0001), revised as Rev A, B, C with the changes in revision clouds, and carries its PDF inside.

  • Acknowledged on purpose, with optional check questions
  • A live acknowledgement register by department for the desk
  • Search reads the text inside attached PDFs
Meeting threads

Every meeting ends with a recap and who does what.

Each meeting keeps one thread for its chat and files, before, during and after. Log an action point from a chat line (/action Ravi to send the GA by Fri) and it lands on the owner's Desk. When the meeting ends, the recap is posted: attendance, recording, action points, decisions, files and chat.

  • Open action points come forward to the next meeting
  • A catch-up card for late joiners
  • Copy the recap, or send it by email to colleagues or guests
Floor plan

The real office, with everyone on their seat.

The office's own seating drawing at scale, every seat with an ID. See who is in today and who works from home, on site or at a client. Find a seat or a person and walk there, or book a hot desk for today or a working day up to two weeks ahead.

  • Office managers move seats in plans that take effect on a set day
  • Everyone who moves is told once, with the walk to their new seat
  • Every change is a revision, compared with revision clouds
Bots together

Bots that ask each other, hand work on and keep a schedule.

A bot can ask another a question ("Is a CAESAR II seat free?") or hand a request on and get it back with a result: the drawing check hands the issued set to the plot room. Each hop shows as a relay card, checked for permission and loops, and audited.

  • Scheduled jobs in plain words: "every weekday at 9:15"
  • Working days and office holidays respected
  • A job sheet with pause, skip, run now and dry run

Security

Your data stays yours. Nobody joins uninvited.

Device keys

Every PC and phone has its own Ed25519 key, sealed by Windows, and by the Android Keystore on phones. Signing in never involves a password.

Approved devices only

An admin approves each new device by matching a code shown on both screens. Lost laptops are removed in one click.

Pinned encryption

In the office all traffic is TLS, pinned to the hub's own certificate. From outside, each device runs its own X25519 and AES-256-GCM channel to the hub, checked against the same certificate, so the relay in between sees only scrambled data.

Private Direct messages

Direct messages and their files are end-to-end encrypted between devices: the hub stores only sealed envelopes and ciphertext. Every admin action is in the audit log.

Questions

Good to know

Does anything go over the internet?

In the office, no: messages, files and sign-ins stay between office PCs and the hub. If the office turns on access from outside, devices away from the office reach the hub through a small relay on the office's own Cloudflare account. Each device has its own encrypted channel to the hub, checked against the hub's certificate, so the relay only ever sees scrambled data. The office opens no ports.

What happens if the hub PC is switched off?

Apps show a reconnecting state and keep everything readable. They reconnect on their own when the hub returns, and follow it if its IP address changes.

Why does Windows SmartScreen warn on first install?

The installers are not yet signed with a paid code-signing certificate. Click More info → Run anyway. You can check every download against SHA256SUMS.txt on the release.

How do updates work?

The app checks when it opens, every few hours and on demand. It downloads in the background from the hub's mirror (or GitHub), verifies the file and installs when you restart.

Can I use it at home or on mobile data?

Yes, once an administrator turns on access from outside the office. Phones and laptops that have joined in the office simply keep working outside. Add a new one from Preferences → Your devices on a PC that is already in: a QR code for a phone, a text code for a laptop. They move back to the office network on their own.

Features

Built like a drafting table, not another chat app.

Everything Dexterity needs from a workplace messenger, with a design language of our own: lenses instead of tabs, a spine timeline, and a floating Dock.

Spaces & Direct

Conversations that read like a revision history.

Messages hang off a single spine in runs, with day marks like a title block. Your own runs carry an amber notch, so you can find your part instantly.

  • Open and private Spaces for teams and projects
  • Direct conversations for one-to-one and small groups
  • Line numbers like 6"-P-1001-A1B are recognised automatically
Side-tracks

Side discussions stay attached to the question.

Reply in a side-track and the conversation keeps its place, visibly connected to the message that started it. Nobody loses the thread.

  • Reactions, pins, edits and deletes
  • @mentions with a personal amber highlight
  • "Seen" receipts and typing indicators
Light & dark

At home on Windows 11, by day and by night.

Mica surfaces, the system's light or dark mode, and a Ctrl+K palette that jumps to any person, Space or command.

  • Pulse shows only what needs you
  • Native notifications that open the right conversation
  • Unread badge on the taskbar
Android

The same office in your pocket.

On the office Wi-Fi your phone finds the hub, links to your PC account and joins every conversation, with the same encryption and approvals.

Rooms

Voice and video calls for up to eight people with screen sharing. The media goes PC to PC over the LAN, so calls are crisp and nothing leaves the office.

Files up to 1 GB

Drag, drop or paste drawings, photos and sheets. Images open in place, everything else opens or saves with one click, and identical files are stored once.

Search everything

Ctrl+K finds people, Spaces, commands and the words inside messages, including line numbers, and jumps straight to the message.

Presence that means something

Available, Busy, Do not disturb or Invisible. You show as away automatically when your PC is idle or locked, and as in a Room during calls.

Security

Nothing leaves the office. Nobody joins uninvited.

Device keys

Every PC and phone has its own Ed25519 key, sealed by Windows. Signing in never involves a password.

Approved devices only

An admin approves each new device by matching a code shown on both screens. Lost laptops are removed in one click.

Pinned encryption

All traffic is TLS, pinned to the hub's own certificate. Sign-in proofs are bound to that certificate, so they can't be replayed.

Audit trail

Every approval, removal and role change is recorded in the Control room.

Set-up

Running in an afternoon.

Install the hub

Unzip it on one always-on PC (for example D:\Dexterity Connect Hub) and double-click Install-Hub.cmd. It becomes a Windows service.

Approve colleagues

As colleagues install, approve them in the Control room by matching their device code.

Questions

Good to know

Does anything go over the internet?

No. Messages, files and sign-ins stay between office PCs and the hub. The only optional internet use is the hub checking this page's releases for updates; offices that never connect can update by hand.

What happens if the hub PC is switched off?

Apps show a reconnecting state and keep everything readable. They reconnect on their own when the hub returns, and follow it if its IP address changes.

Why does Windows SmartScreen warn on first install?

The installers are not yet signed with a paid code-signing certificate. Click More info → Run anyway. You can check every download against SHA256SUMS.txt on the release.

How do updates work?

The app checks when it opens, every few hours and on demand. It downloads in the background from the hub's mirror (or GitHub), verifies the file and installs when you restart.

Can phones use it outside the office?

Phones connect while they are on the office Wi-Fi. The design keeps everything inside the office network on purpose.

Dexterity Connect

Pair your phone

This adds Dexterity Connect on this device to your office account. It works once, for 24 hours from when your PC made it.

Get Dexterity Connect

The office messenger of Dexterity Design Services. To use it on your own phone or laptop, open Preferences → Your devices on your office PC and add it from there.

Open Dexterity Connect for iPhone
  1. Tap Open Dexterity Connect for iPhone. It opens in Safari, from your office's own server.
  2. Follow it to copy your code and Add to Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen).
  3. Open Dexterity Connect from the Home Screen and tap Paste the code.

There is nothing to download from the App Store: on iPhone, Dexterity Connect is a web app your office serves itself. Notifications need iOS 16.4 or later.

Download for Android Already installed? Open the app
  1. Tap Download for Android, then open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser.
  2. Open Dexterity Connect. It picks up your code by itself and signs you in.
